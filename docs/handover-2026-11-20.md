## 6. Open problem and suspects (in the order I would check them)
A. **DU1 and DU2 may not be time-synchronous in the hub.** A real TDD network is synchronous (SSBs of all cells
   at the same time). If the DUs were aligned, the neighbour SSB would be at the SERVING SSB timing, the
   time-domain search would not be needed, and patch #1 already handles exactly overlapping SSBs (simulated).
   Check first (read-only): in the same UE buffer, where is the serving SSB (PCI 0 for UE1)? Add a diag line
   that runs `pss_search_time_nr()` for the serving PCI on the same slot and logs its position. Is 4560 the
   serving SSB? Then DU2 is ~2900 samples (~95 us) late.
   Why DUs could be offset: gNB RF path sets frame = timestamp / samples_per_frame (`executables/nr-ru.c:175`),
   `ru->ts_offset` starts at 0 (`nr-ru.c:940`) but is moved on start-up timing jumps (`nr-ru.c:158-166`).
   The hub gives each DU the hub time at connect (DU2 connects later). Check DU logs / hub handshake.
   If offset: fix in the hub (align each DU's time base to a frame boundary) or in the DU start-up.
   Side effect of an offset: TDD DL/UL of the two cells overlap wrongly (cross-link interference); this may
   also affect the 19 Nov cross-cell interference numbers.
B. **SSS check too permissive** for a single known PCI: `rx_sss_nr()` (`sss_nr.c`) with nb_nid1 = 1, 15 phase
   tries, metric/base >= SSS_METRIC_FLOOR_NR (3). Passes at partial-overlap timings. Options: require the
   candidate's per-cell coherence (|sum z[n-1] z*[n]| / sum |r|^2) above a floor; or compare against the
   SSS metric of the serving PCI at the same timing; or add PBCH DMRS check of the known PCI (PR #919 has
   `nr_compute_normalized_dmrs()`, real cell 0.95-0.99, wrong 0.04-0.22) as a second gate.
C. **Strong serving cell dominates the PSS correlation** (10 dB stronger): its peaks fill the top-8 list.
   Options: exclude timings where the serving PCI's PSS correlates strongly; or cancel the serving cell's
   known PSS/SSS before the neighbour search (interference cancellation, as real UEs do); or once the cell
   is located, always include the tracked position as a candidate.
D. Offline model (not lab-verified): OAI's SSS check uses a per-subcarrier PSS channel estimate
   (`pss_ch_est_nr()`); with an exactly overlapping 10 dB stronger cell it fails for neighbours >= 6 dB weaker.
   Smoothing the estimate over ~15 subcarriers fixed it in the model up to ~6-10 dB.
Data to collect: per candidate log pos, PSS dB, SSS pass, per-cell RSRP, coherence; serving SSB position.

## 9. Result (20 Nov evening) - replaces sections 3, 5, 6, 7

### 9.1 Root cause
- The DUs are time-synchronous (section 2). The neighbour SSB lies exactly on the serving SSB (PSS at sample 4560
  of slot 0). Suspect A in its original form was wrong; its consequence was right: no time-domain search is needed.
- Every PSS/SSS position chosen by #6/#7 (5000-8000) was wrong: the FFT window there overlaps part of the SSB,
  so the SSS check passes by accident (suspect B).
- The per-cell RSRP of patch #1 is exact at the serving timing (lab rho 0.087 / 0.195 / 0.333 / 0.497 at
  10 / 6 / 3 / 0 dB; theory 0.091 / 0.20 / 0.333 / 0.5).
- A first CFAR test without cancellation failed from ~6 dB: the product of the serving SSS and the neighbour SSS
  gives every other PCI a leftover that grows with the square root of the neighbour power (max dummy rho 0.014
  absent, 0.055 at 10 dB, 0.11 at 0 dB). Changing PCIs does not fix this (model: only 19 of 59 PCIs pass at
  10 dB, the best fail at 20 dB, and they all have the same PCI mod 3 as DU1).

`oai-ue-neigh-known-pci.patch` for a known neighbour PCI on the serving SSB frequency:
1. Check at the serving SSB timing first (`serving_ssb_pss_pos()`: `fp->ssb_index`, `nr_get_ssb_start_symbol()`,
   CP rule of `nr_slot_fep()`; -1 in slots without serving SSB). Fallback: the old PSS/SSS search of PR #919
   (`validate_known_pci()`), for other SSB positions, cells that are not synchronous, FDD. Both use the same CFAR test.
2. CFAR test: remove the serving SSS from a copy of the SSS symbol (timing ramp, channel = mean over 15
   subcarriers), then ratio = rho(neighbour) / max rho of 6 unused PCIs with the same N_ID2 (PCI 1: 4, 7, ...,
   19; PCI 0: 3, 6, ..., 18; serving PCI and listed neighbours are excluded).
3. Hysteresis: add a new cell at ratio > 3 on 2 checks in a row (a check that tests no timing does not break the
   series); keep a valid cell while ratio > 2.0; LOST after 10 failed checks in a row, then INT_MAX to RRC
   (RRC resets the cell: `rrc_UE.c:3380`).
4. `last_meas_slot` is updated on every check. Before, a valid cell that failed was checked again in every
   slot 0 (also in frames without SSB): 10 failures took ~0.1 s, and one miss could become LOST.
5. Reported RSRP: unchanged (patch #1, from the signal without cancellation). Cancellation is used for detection only.
6. Diag lines (`[e2e-ts diag] ... ADDED / confirmed / not detected / LOST`): off by default. Switch:
   `E2E_NEIGH_DIAG=1 tools/lab-up.sh` (`lab-up.sh` and `UE/run-ue.sh` pass it through sudo). The UE logs
   `neighbour diag lines on|off` once. The switch is read when the UE starts.

Schedule (PR #1142, `is_neighboring_cell_due()`): a valid cell is checked only in its `ssb_slot`, every 200 ms.
A cell that is not valid (at start, after LOST) is checked every 21 slots in any slot (search mode), so most of
those checks find no SSB and test no timing (lab, 60 dB: 19 of 566 diag lines of UE1 tested a timing, ~3 %).
